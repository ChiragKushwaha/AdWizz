What is AdWizz?

AdWizz is a free, open-source ad blocker for Android that removes ads system-wide: in apps, games and every browser, including Chrome and Samsung Internet. It works as an on-device DNS ad blocker, like having a Pi-hole inside your phone, so it needs no root and no account. It also blocks pop-up ads, redirect ads, trackers and malware sites, and your browsing never leaves your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Google Play doesn't allow apps that block ads in other apps. That's why ad blockers like this are distributed directly. Every release here is built from the open-source code on GitHub and signed with the same key, and Android refuses updates signed by anyone else.

Is it really a VPN? Does my traffic go somewhere?

It uses Android's VPN feature only to see DNS look-ups (the "phone book" of the internet) on your own device. Ad and tracker names get a "doesn't exist" answer; everything else goes straight to your chosen DNS server. Your browsing never passes through anyone else's server.

Why do some YouTube, Instagram or Prime Video ads still show?

Those ads come from the same servers as the videos and posts themselves (streaming apps like Prime Video and JioHotstar stitch the ad into the video stream), so blocking them would break the app. Every DNS-based blocker has this limit. AdWizz already blocks their separate ad and tracking servers.

Why is there an empty space where an ad was?

The app reserved room for the ad before trying to load it. AdWizz stops the ad from arriving, but it can't change another app's layout, so some apps leave the gap.

How does the Reels & Shorts blocker work?

Reels and Shorts come from the same servers as the rest of Instagram and YouTube, so DNS can't block them. Instead, AdWizz uses Android's Accessibility service to check which screen those apps are showing and presses Back when it's a short-video player. It only watches those apps, doesn't read what's on screen, and keeps nothing.

Ads still show in my browser

Check that Android's Private DNS is set to Off or Automatic, and that Chrome's "Use secure DNS" isn't set to a custom provider. AdWizz shows a warning if Private DNS is bypassing it.

Can it protect devices on my hotspot?

Android never routes hotspot traffic through a VPN, but AdWizz's Devices screen shows a QR code that sets up ad-blocking DNS on iPhones, Macs and other devices in a few taps.

Privacy

AdWizz has no account and no servers of its own. The list of blocked and allowed domains is kept only in memory on your phone, and your browsing and DNS look-ups are never sent anywhere. It downloads public blocklists and checks this site for updates.

To find bugs and learn how the app is used, AdWizz sends anonymous usage statistics, crash reports and performance data (such as app opens, start-up time and the device model) to Google Firebase. It never sends the sites or apps you use, and the advertising ID is turned off.
